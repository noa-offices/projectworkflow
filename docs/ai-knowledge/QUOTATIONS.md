## Specification / PDF / presentation

[Specification page](../../app/quotations/%5Bid%5D/specification/page.tsx), [specification download](../../app/quotations/%5Bid%5D/download-specification/route.ts), [PDF page](../../app/quotations/%5Bid%5D/pdf/page.tsx), [PDF download](../../app/quotations/%5Bid%5D/download-pdf/route.ts), [presentation page](../../app/quotations/%5Bid%5D/presentation/page.tsx), [PPTX endpoint](../../app/api/quotations/%5Bid%5D/presentation-pptx/route.ts), [PPTX helper](../../lib/quotations/presentation-pptx.ts), and [PDF generator](../../lib/server/generate-pdf-buffer.ts). Settings APIs and document tables are separate per document; inspect the relevant endpoint and settings store before changes. Related documents include purchase order, RFQ, order confirmation and delivery note under quotation routes. Tests: [presentation settings](../../lib/quotations/presentation-settings.test.mts), [specification contract](../../lib/quotations/final-specification-ai-contract.test.mts). Complete rendering equivalence and deployment dependencies: **NOT VERIFIED**.

Quotation PDF totals visibility is stored per quotation in `quotation_pdfs.settings_json` as `showItemDiscount` and `showExtraDiscount`; omitted keys default to true. The PDF preview editor saves these through [PDF settings store](../../lib/quotations/quotation-pdf-settings-store.ts), separate from `layout_settings.columns` for line table visibility. The local builder publishes price rounding metadata in `layout_settings.price_rounding`; its additive `adjustment` is display information and does not change saved totals.
